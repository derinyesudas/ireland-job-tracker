Surface the white-collar roles she can actually get

The role families were already right; their weights were not. Customer
operations sat at 12 and general admin at 10 against 34 for insurance
operations, so a customer service or administrator job could never reach a
band she would look at. Any title the families did not recognise was capped
at 35 - the top of "stretch" - so an unusually named but perfectly applicable
role could not surface however well the rest of the advert matched.

Raised customer operations to 26, general admin to 22, quality process to 24,
project support to 20, and the unrecognised-title ceiling to 48. Added the
title wordings those families were missing: customer advisor, client service
executive, member services, policyholder services, administrator on its own,
team administrator, annuity and scheme administrator.

These live in scraper/score.py rather than the encrypted profile, whose
passphrase is lost. The profile still wins wherever it is more generous, so
nothing here lowers a weight that was set deliberately.

Measured against the live board with the real profile: 22 jobs rise, none
fall. Canada Life's Customer Service Representative - which pays for the QFA
- goes 44 to 58, Davy's Client Service Executive 17 to 52, WTW's Client
Service Advisor 29 to 55, Deloitte's Administrator 17 to 48. All were sitting
in the weak band.

Also closes a gap in the eligibility filter: warehouse, production,
manufacturing and bioprocess associate titles now block as production-floor
roles rather than reading as office work.
