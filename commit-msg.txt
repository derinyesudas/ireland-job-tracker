Mark Critical Skills roles, drop the redundant Graduate pill

The "Graduate programme" pill repeated the job title six inches to its left,
so it is gone. Internship and Entry level pills stay - those are not always
in the title.

Adds scraper/csol.py: the 2026 Critical Skills Occupations List from the
Department of Enterprise, as matching rules.

The list is narrower than it looks. "Business analysts" is not on it; what is
on it is "management consultants and business analysts specialising in big
data analytics with skills in IT, data mining, modelling, and advanced maths".
Matching on title alone would tag every Business Analyst advert in Ireland and
most would not qualify, so each rule needs the title AND the specialisation
the entry names. The pill's tooltip quotes the CSOL line it matched, the
words in the advert that satisfied it, and the SOC-4 code.

Salary floor is EUR 36,848. A stated salary below it suppresses the tag. Only
9% of live adverts state a salary at all, so requiring one would mean the tag
never appeared: a job with no stated salary is tagged and says "salary n/a"
rather than implying a threshold was checked.

12 of 665 live jobs tag today, across four CSOL codes - accountants and
taxation, actuaries and statisticians, IT systems analysts, quality assurance
and regulatory.
