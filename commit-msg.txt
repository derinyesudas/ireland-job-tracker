Search the job title, not the whole advert

Typing "pension" returned 155 results, because the box searched every word of
every description - including the benefits paragraph of roles with nothing to
do with pensions. It now searches the job title first and the company name
second, and nothing else.

  pension           155 results -> 2      (both titled Pensions Administrator)
  customer service   59 results -> 2
  claims             25 results -> 10
  analyst            73 results -> 51
  davy               13 results -> 13     (matched on company, as before)

A title hit always ranks above a company hit, whatever sort is selected, so
searching a role name puts that role at the top rather than whichever job
happens to score highest.
