Score the white-collar roles she is actually applying for

The scorer only knew about analyst families, so pensions administration,
claims, customer service, back office and general office work scored near
zero no matter how good a fit they were - and sat in the weak band where she
never saw them.

scripts/retune.py adds five role families to the encrypted profile:

  pensions_insurance_admin  30  the job she has already held
  financial_ops_admin       26  fund admin, settlements, payroll, accounts
  client_service            22  contact centre, customer and client support
  office_support            18  administrator, coordinator, business support
  sales_support             16  broker and intermediary support

and a "training provided" bonus, because an advert saying experience is not
required is the shape of job that is open to someone with one year behind her.

The profile is encrypted, so this runs locally with the vault key. It writes
nothing blind: it prints the change, re-scores the live board both ways so the
movement is visible, and asks before saving. It keeps a backup of the previous
profile, and if the board cannot be scored it says so loudly rather than
reporting a reassuring "0 jobs moved".
